16 March 1903 · Laurier & World War I (1897-1919)

High Commissioner Lord Strathcona's cipher cable to Laurier on Australia's Eastern Extension deal

Lord Strathcona, High Commissioner for Canada (London) to Sir Wilfrid Laurier, Prime Minister of Canada (Ottawa) · 16 March 1903

Government & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View historical publication on Internet Archive ↗

Historical context

Documents Canada's encrypted diplomatic battle to protect the newly completed All-Red Line Pacific Cable from rival private cable concessions in Australia.

Decoded message (plain text)

Do you wish me to take any action respecting extension facilities the Australian Commonwealth proposes to grant Eastern Extension Company? New Zealand instructed its Agent General to join Imperial and Dominion governments in making strong protest. Laurier replied 18 March: Have made strong protest to Barton; do everything to defeat contemplated arrangement.

Original secret code

London-to-Ottawa cipher telegram of 16 March 1903 ('On the 16th instant I telegraphed you in cypher as follows').
