8 May 1759 · New France & Early Canada (1673-1815)

Vaudreuil's encrypted crisis dispatch on Québec's supply shortage weeks before Wolfe's siege

Marquis de Vaudreuil, Governor General of New France (Montréal) to Nicolas-René Berryer, Minister of the Marine (Versailles) · 8 May 1759

War & Spies
Open interactive decoder → View original manuscript at New France Archives (ANOM) ↗

Historical context

Sent on 8 May 1759, just before Bougainville returned from France with news that Versailles could spare no major reinforcements.

Decoded message (plain text)

Intendant Bigot prepared a courier vessel to carry duplicates to France; Vaudreuil describes the desperate state of provisions and urgent defensive preparations for Québec before British fleets enter the St. Lawrence.
