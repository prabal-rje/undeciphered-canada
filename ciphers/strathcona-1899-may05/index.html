Strathcona warns Britain will resist joint Pacific Cable ownership

Lord Strathcona (London) to PM Sir Wilfrid Laurier (Ottawa), 5 May 1899

Decoded message (plain text)

Very confidential. Pacific Cable. In view Chamberlain letter do not think Government will accept principle joint ownership and control. Recommend endeavour [...] them [...] us and Australasia guarantee [interest] and sinking fund for stipulated period to permit raising money on most favourable terms. If they consent and do not impose onerous conditions their further co-operation not indispensable. If you concur better authorize me state Canada can not accept present proposal and to suggest joint guarantee letting it be understood that we can only proceed on principle of joint ownership or joint guarantee. Strathcona.
