Ottawa-Vancouver Secret Code Telegram: McInnes ('Brown') to David C. Lew on Deputy Minister O'Hara ('Hudson')

1910-09-21

Government & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View historical publication on Internet Archive ↗

Historical context

Sent from Ottawa on 21 September 1910 by special agent T. R. E. McInnes to Vancouver Customs interpreter David C. Lew during the federal investigation into Chinese head-tax immigration frauds and opium smuggling. The telegram employs the Ottawa-Vancouver secret codebook ('Hudson' = Deputy Minister F. C. T. O'Hara) exposed in Justice Denis Murphy's 1911 Royal Commission report.

Decoded message (plain text)

Hearty congratulations, F. C. T. O'Hara (Deputy Minister of Trade and Commerce) delighted. Action certain.

Original secret code

Hearty congratulations, Hudson delighted. Action certain.
