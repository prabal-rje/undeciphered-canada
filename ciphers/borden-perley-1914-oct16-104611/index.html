16 October 1914 · Laurier & World War I (1897-1919)

Early wartime Borden-Perley secret cipher establishes the standing -1110 London key

Prime Minister Sir Robert L. Borden (Ottawa) to Sir George H. Perley, Acting High Commissioner (London) · 16 October 1914

War & Spies
Open interactive decoder → View original microfilm at Library and Archives Canada / Héritage Canadiana ↗

Historical context

Demonstrates that the -1110 shift used by Borden in November 1916 to inform Perley of Militia Minister Sam Hughes's dismissal had been in continuous bilateral service for over two years.

Decoded message (plain text)

Summary of paired draft (folio 104613): Borden cables Perley on war-material procurement, noting Watt has no material on hand but manufacturers can supply it on short notice, and asking Perley to verify indirect reports of a War Office purchasing representative.

Original secret code

Secret marital expatiatory vaccination has penuriousness nick bread...
