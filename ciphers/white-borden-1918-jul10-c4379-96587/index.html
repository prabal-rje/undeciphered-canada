10 July 1918 · Laurier & World War I (1897-1919)

Post-Halifax Explosion dry dock syndicate: Acting PM Thomas White wires Borden in newly discovered Slater -120

Acting Prime Minister Sir Thomas White (Ottawa) to Prime Minister Sir Robert L. Borden (London) · 10 July 1918

Government & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View original microfilm at Library and Archives Canada / Héritage Canadiana ↗

Historical context

Sent seven months after the December 1917 Halifax Explosion destroyed much of the harbour waterfront, Acting Prime Minister Sir Thomas White used a distinct Slater -120 key to warn Borden in London against granting British shipbuilder Beardmore Ltd. an option on the Halifax graving dock while Ballantyne assembled a Canadian syndicate.

Decoded message (plain text)

Opposing Beardmore Ltd. option on Halifax graving and floating dock seven months after the Halifax Explosion; Marine Minister C. C. Ballantyne is arranging a new syndicate for the Halifax shipbuilding plant and profits tax understanding.

Original secret code

456. Ballantyne informs me Beardmore Ltd. seeking option on Halifax graving dock. Notable syndicate being formed by Ballantyne for Halifax shipbuilding plant. Offering to give option would be very undesirable. Propitiators theist unlike mangold inquisitorial.
