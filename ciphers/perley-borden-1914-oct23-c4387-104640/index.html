23 October 1914 · Laurier & World War I (1897-1919)

Dominion Police Chief Percy Sherwood and New York munitions rumours: Perley cables Borden in Slater -1110

Sir George H. Perley, Acting Canadian High Commissioner (London) to Prime Minister Sir Robert L. Borden (Ottawa) · 23 October 1914

War & Spies

Historical context

In a second secret Slater -1110 cablegram the same day, Perley suggested deploying Dominion Police Chief Commissioner Sir Percy Sherwood (Canada's wartime counter-intelligence chief) to investigate rumours of Col. Allison's wartime purchasing activities in New York.

Decoded message (plain text)

Secret. Rumours circulating here from New York regarding purchases there by Allison for Canadian Government and others. Possibly Sherwood might secure some information there.

Original secret code

Secret sines circulating infuse hell peerless ambitiously salmon repayings vital club allison hall coldness immortalize and others quenchless sher potsdam obstreperous spends thickly lapidary there.
