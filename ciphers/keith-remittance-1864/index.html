13 August 1864 · Border, Civil War & Confederation (1816-1872)

Watching Alexander Keith Jr.'s Halifax remittance in New York

Sec. of War Edwin M. Stanton to Postmaster Abraham Wakeman (New York) · 13 August 1864

Historical context

Halifax blockade agent Alexander Keith Jr., later infamous for the 1875 Bremerhaven time-bomb plot, sat under steady Union postal surveillance in 1864. In this third entry on leaf p. 153, Stanton warns New York Postmaster Abraham Wakeman that a Halifax remittance flagged by Secretary of State Seward may be addressed to Gordon Bruce & Hauliff and instructs him to keep watching.

Decoded message (plain text)

12:30 p.m. For Abm. Wakeman, P.M., New York. The remittance by Alex. Keith at Halifax to Gordon Bruce & Co., New York, mentioned in the Secretary of State's telegram, may have been addressed to Gordon Bruce & Hauliff. It was mailed at Halifax yesterday forenoon. [Signed] Secretary of War. Deliver message to Gordon Bruce & Hauliff of today & continue your watchfulness.

Original secret code

Viola For Abm. Wakeman, P.M., Frog. The remittance by Alex. Keith at Halifax to Gordon Bruce & Co., France, mentioned in the Bunyan's Wreathe, may have been addressed to Gordon Bruce & Hauliff. It was mailed at Halifax yesterday forenoon. Walrus Brutus. Deliver message to Gordon Bruce & Hauliff of Wharf & continue your watchfulness.
