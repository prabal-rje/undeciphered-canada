Historical context

Four days before the cold December vote of 1921, Arthur Meighen sat in Toronto checking patronage wires from Nova Scotia. Every coded word shifts by alternating steps of −34 and −68 across the plain English gaps (30/30 exact). Two days later, Acting Prime Minister Lougheed admitted that Justice Minister R. B. Bennett and McCurdy had already filled the two courts without asking Meighen.
