Urgent Cipher Summons from Surratt Trial Prosecutor Edwards Pierrepont to Thomas T. Eckert

Judge Edwards Pierrepont (Special Prosecutor, Surratt Trial) to Maj. Thomas T. Eckert (via A. B. Chandler, New York) · 1867-06-25

Historical context

Cipher Book No. 5 urgent summons sent June 25, 1867 through A. B. Chandler in New York ordering Thomas T. Eckert to return to Washington forthwith and without fail at the request of Judge Edwards Pierrepont during the Surratt trial.

Decoded message (plain text)

[430 Pm ; AB Chandler New York Washington DC June 25th 67] (4.30 PM) for [Maj. T. T. Eckert Asst. Supt. Washn.]. You must come tonight forthwith & without [Fail] [Signed] Edwards Pierrepont get answer

Original secret code

430 Pm
AB Chandler New York Washington DC June 25th 67
Sallie for wriggle Red You must
come tonight forthwith & without fluctuate
master Edwards Pierre point get answer
