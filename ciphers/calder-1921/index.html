18 March 1921 · Interwar Ottawa (1920-1938)

The ink-blacked names behind the 1921 Royal Grain Inquiry

PM Arthur Meighen to Hon. J. A. Calder (Regina) · 18 March 1921

Unread until nowGovernment & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View original scan at Library and Archives Canada / Héritage Canadiana ↗

Historical context

When Meighen sought a chairman for the 1921 Royal Grain Inquiry Commission, Immigration Minister J. A. Calder first wired "UNABLE DECIPHER YOUR WIRE" and then sent a clear veto with two names blotted out under thick black ink. Applying the file's split-channel key wire (#032377) restores them: Saskatchewan Chief Justice James T. Brown and Justice H. V. Bigelow.

Decoded message (plain text)

“Desire Saskatchewan judge for chairman grain inquiry. Stop. Brown or Bigelow suggested. Stop. Wire your advice immediately. Stop. Other members probably Wood and Staples. Stop. Therefore must be very careful of chairman.”

Original secret code

Raw ciphertext wire at C-3436 p. 1197 (#032378), zero decode on file.
