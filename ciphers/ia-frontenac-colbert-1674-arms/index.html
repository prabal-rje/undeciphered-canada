14 November 1674 · New France & Early Canada (1673-1815)

Frontenac's cipher warning on the scarcity of arms and ammunition in New France

Louis de Buade, Comte de Frontenac, Governor of New France (Québec) to Jean-Baptiste Colbert, Minister of the Marine (Versailles) · 14 November 1674

War & Spies
Open interactive decoder → View historical publication on Internet Archive ↗

Historical context

Alarmed by Dutch raiders seizing French posts in Acadia in 1674, Frontenac used cipher to conceal Canada's acute powder and musket shortage.

Decoded message (plain text)

I find it very difficult to constrain the settlers to keep arms, powder, and lead, as much from their poverty as on account of the scarcity of arms and ammunition existing this year in the Country, where only three vessels have arrived and brought scarcely any.

Original secret code

90 - 20. 21. 39. 69 18
