14 August 1785 · New France & Early Canada (1673-1815)

Monroe's encrypted Congressional dispatch to Madison on commercial treaties and British trade restraints

James Monroe, Delegate to Congress (New York) to James Madison (Virginia) · 14 August 1785

Diplomacy & Empires
Open interactive decoder → View historical publication on Internet Archive ↗

Historical context

Illustrates early post-Revolution economic rivalry across the British North American border.

Decoded message (plain text)

Reports a pending Congressional committee report revising commercial treaty instructions to counter British trade restrictions affecting Canada, the West Indies, and Atlantic shipping.

Original secret code

175. 428. 438. 508. 430. 589. 386. 296. 430. 331. 233. 170. 75. 230. 177. 89. 21. 473. 3. 200. 40. 430. ... 343. 573. the 297. 122. 414. 302. 263. 508. ... 301. 430. 430. 40. 430. 202. 330. 430. 250.
