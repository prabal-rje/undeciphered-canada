November 1871 · Border, Civil War & Confederation (1816-1872)

R. W. Scott's explicit Caesar shift instruction ('using S for A') in Macdonald's Ontario cabinet intrigue

Richard William Scott (Ottawa / Toronto) to Sir John A. Macdonald (Ottawa) & Sir Francis Hincks · November 1871

Government & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View historical publication on Internet Archive ↗

Historical context

Rare explicit documentation of a simple Caesar shift cipher ('S for A') used in post-Confederation Canadian political telegraphy before Slater's Code became ubiquitous.

Decoded message (plain text)

Scott asks Macdonald to consult Sir Francis Hincks privately about Scott joining the Ontario government and to telegraph his advice in Caesar cipher; Macdonald and Hincks send a cipher reply declining to advise against taking office.

Original secret code

Key directive: 'Telegraph me to-morrow, in cypher, using S for A.'
