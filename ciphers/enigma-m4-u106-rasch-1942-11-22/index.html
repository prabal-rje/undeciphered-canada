U-106 (Kptlt. Hermann Rasch) North Atlantic Enigma M4 Convoy-Supply Intercept (Grid BB 25)

U-106 (Hermann Rasch) to B.d.U. (German U-boat Command) · 1942-11-22

Historical context

Authentic WWII Kriegsmarine Triton (Shark) M4 intercept from Kapitaenleutnant Hermann Rasch aboard Atlantic U-boat U-106 reporting fuel, provisions, and rendezvous coordinates; the operator's transmitted naval grid BB 25 falls directly off western Newfoundland in the Gulf of St. Lawrence approaches.

Decoded message (plain text)

Boat clear. [Will rendezvous] at Schnoor about 27 November. 60 cubic meters [fuel oil remaining], provisions [sufficient] until 20 December. Need binoculars; [only] four now serviceable. Am at naval square BB 25. Situation like Schaefer’s. [Wind] northwest [force] 5, [atmospheric pressure] 15 millibars rising, good [...] [Machine decrypt: BOOTKLARXBEIJSCHNOORBETWAZWOSIBENXNOVXSECHSNULCBMXPROVIANTBISZWONULXDEZXBENOETIGEGLMESERYNOCHVIEFKLHRXSTEHEMAR...]
