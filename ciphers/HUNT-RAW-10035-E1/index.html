Historical context

June 22, 1865 Cipher Book No. 1 directive sent simultaneously to every Military Division and Department cipher operator across the Northern, Eastern, Northwest, and Pacific commands.

Decoded message (plain text)

[No 1 8 PM ; All Cipher Oprs at Mil Div & Dep. Hdqrs Washn June 22 / 65] [Washington] (8 PM) [June 22] for All mil [Division] and [Department] [Command = Er (-ed, -ing)]ers, copy to each. confidential circular. all [Department] [Command = Er (-ed, -ing)]ers, [Command = Er (-ed, -ing)]ing in states where martial law prevails will immediately put detectives upon the [Surrender] for gambling houses especially faro banks and [At the] appropriate time make a decent upon them all simultaneously [Arresting] all disbursing officers [Of the] [Government] who may be found gambling in them or visitants there in [At the] time and who it canby proven had previously gambled at such places. The gambling institute shuns wilby completely broken up and their money and stock confiscated & the owners or proprietors of such game billing institute shuns be made to disgorge & refund all money they may have [1] from [Unite]d states disbursing officers. The officers so taken wilby imprisoned and tried immy. ditto same proceedings wilby taken by [Department] [Command = Er (-ed, -ing)]ers [In the] [North] with in their respective [Command = Er (-ed, -ing)]'s [In the] cities where disbursing officers may be located except that instead of confiscating the money & stock [Of the] game billing establish meant or compelling by military action the owners or proprietors [Of the] same to disgorge or refund any money they may have [1] from disbursing officers [Of the] [Government] They will be immy [Report]ed to the civil authorities for their action. this wilby kept strictly confident shall except so far as it may be [Necessary] to [Communicate] it to those who are to carry it into execution [Signed] [Maj Genl U.S. Grant] be apt to spoil their business 215
