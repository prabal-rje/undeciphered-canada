3 November 1758 · New France & Early Canada (1673-1815)

Vaudreuil's encrypted letter on Doreil and Bougainville's mission to Versailles

Marquis de Vaudreuil, Governor General of New France (Montréal) to Claude-Louis d'Espinchal, Marquis de Massiac (Versailles) · 3 November 1758

Diplomacy & Empires
Open interactive decoder → View original manuscript at New France Archives (ANOM) ↗

Historical context

Companion to Vaudreuil's same-day defence memorandum (item 27392), showing Vaudreuil trying to manage how Bougainville and Doreil would represent the colony's plight at Versailles.

Decoded message (plain text)

Explains the departure of André Doreil and Louis-Antoine de Bougainville for France to plead for emergency reinforcements while anticipating multi-front British attacks after Louisbourg's fall.
