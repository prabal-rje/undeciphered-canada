11 April 1890 · Sir John A. Macdonald Era (1873-1896)

Bering Sea sealing arbitration: Macdonald instructs C. H. Tupper in Washington in unglossed Slater -600

Prime Minister Sir John A. Macdonald (Ottawa) to Hon. Charles Hibbert Tupper, Minister of Marine and Fisheries (Washington, D.C.) · 11 April 1890

Historical context

During tense 1890 Washington negotiations over U.S. seizures of British Columbia sealing vessels in the Bering Sea, Macdonald cabled Marine and Fisheries Minister Charles Hibbert Tupper at the Hotel Arno instructing strict adherence to Canada's draft proposal.

Decoded message (plain text)

Council wish to impress [upon] [representatives?] their desire to adhere as closely as possible to the lines of our proposal.

Original secret code

Decipher atlantic unprincipled insist thieving patriot species laudatory platitude giblets under dissyllable to ancestral as condition [as] propagandist to the marshalling of percipient raffling.
