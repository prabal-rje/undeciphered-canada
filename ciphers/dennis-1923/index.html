9 November 1923 · Interwar Ottawa (1920-1938)

"Please wire me code you use": a baffled publisher in Halifax

Arthur Meighen (Ottawa) to W. H. Dennis, Halifax Herald · 9 November 1923

Unread until nowGovernment & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View original scan at Library and Archives Canada / Héritage Canadiana ↗

Historical context

Minutes after receiving this by-election wire about sending J. B. M. Baxter to Halifax, publisher W. H. Dennis wired back: "PLEASE WIRE ME CODE YOU USE AND NUMBER." Subtracting 63 restores the message and gives a second independent proof that Zornwhite stood for F. B. McCurdy.

Decoded message (plain text)

“Will gladly send Baxter. Stop. Think better get [Zornwhite = F. B. McCurdy] approval this step first. Wire me.”

Original secret code

Will gloze serf Baxter. Stop. Throw better glacis Zornwhite archetype this stoning flatter. Wire me.
