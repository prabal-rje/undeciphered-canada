Historical context

Key-verified Cipher No. 1 twin of 9187 e#1, enabling mathematical recovery of Cipher Book No. 3 vocabulary on the Buffalo northern-frontier telegraph wire.

Decoded message (plain text)

Washington, Feb. 18 [Gradual], 1865, 6 P.M. [Libby]. To Captain [Pilgrim] H. C. Hodges, Buffalo, New York [Amos]. Repeated 8 P.M. [Nancy] 19th [Hunkey] care Brevoort House, New York [France]. You will proceed without delay via New York [Frog], consulting with General [Shelby] Van Vliet [vain fleet], to Beaufort [bible] Harbor [soap] and assume charge of the [torch] supplies collecting there for Maj. Gen. Sherman's [Knapsacks] Army [opal], to carry out such instructions as you may receive from the [Saxon] Quartermaster General U.S. [Bender] or the Chief Quartermaster [Vinton] of Sherman's [Kidnaps] Army [Oyster] [or] from Maj. Gen. Schofield [Kiss], but until the arrival of General [Shelter] Eaton to be specially in charge of the [torch] depot [Queenly] near [France?] Beaufort [Basement] for Sherman's [Kitchens] supplies. You will take with you some clerks and workmen [work spit] to establish the depot [Quotient] immediately on your arrival at Beaufort [Bible] Harbor [Soap]. Signed [yoke], [Betcher/Meigs].

Original secret code
