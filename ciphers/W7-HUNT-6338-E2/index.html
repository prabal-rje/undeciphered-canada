1867-03-14 · Border, Civil War & Confederation (1816-1872)

Top-secret Pacific Special Cipher asking Gen. Halleck for commercial and political valuation of Russian America (Alaska) 16 days before the Alaska Purchase Treaty

War Department (transmitted by Charles A. Tinker for Secretary of War / Cabinet) to James Gamble, San Francisco, for Maj. Gen. Henry W. Halleck · 1867-03-14

Historical context

Sent 16 days before Secretary Seward signed the $7.2 million Alaska Purchase Treaty on March 30, 1867! Remarkably, recipient James Gamble (California State Telegraph Superintendent) is the exact same telegraph executive who negotiated the British Columbia telegraph branch wire in UVic Colonial Despatch B64262 (Oct. 1864). Directly triggered Maj. Gen. Henry W. Halleck's March 18, 1867 reply (4894-4895) estimating Alaska's political value at $5 to $10 million.

Decoded message (plain text)

Washington, March 14, 1867. Special. James Gamble, San Francisco. [Hagar] March [Wooster] for [Alkali Gallon]. Please favor me with your estimate [spark] value to the [Plato] [spark] Russian Possessions in [Sexton] America as designated on map number [weigh] of Colton's Universal Atlas, together with the [quick] of Saint Lawrence and all the [Queries] on the [Swallow] coast and including the [Quicks] in Behring's Straits and the Aleutian [Queries]. In forming your estimate you will take into view all advantages of such acquisition, political as well as commercial, and a title free from all claims of Russian Companies for trading or other purposes. Please consider this as strictly confidential and answer in cipher at your earliest convenience. [unity] [Africa]. Gamble, [Amos] holds us personally responsible for the secrecy of this. Please answer when received and understood. Chas. A. Tinker.
