5 April 1919 · Laurier & World War I (1897-1919)

War Measures Act emergency warning: White wires Borden in Slater -401 that signing peace will automatically expire wartime orders

Acting Prime Minister Sir Thomas White (Ottawa) to Prime Minister Sir Robert L. Borden, Hotel Astoria (Paris) · 5 April 1919

Government & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View original microfilm at Library and Archives Canada / Héritage Canadiana ↗

Historical context

Facing a legal cliff if an early Versailles peace proclamation abruptly terminated Canada's wartime economic and security Orders-in-Council under the 1914 War Measures Act, Acting Prime Minister White urgently queried Borden in Slater -401 before the Senate adjourned.

Decoded message (plain text)

P.265. From White. Confidential. Today's press despatches suggest preliminary peace treaty may be signed early date. Council anxious to know if followed by proclamation declaring peace. Justice Department advises all Orders-in-Council under the War Measures Act would probably automatically come to an end, requiring an emergency bill during the session.
