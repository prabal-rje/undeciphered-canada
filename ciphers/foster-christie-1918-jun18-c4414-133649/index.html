18 June 1918 · Laurier & World War I (1897-1919)

Six-page WWI Canadian manpower reinforcement ledger: unglossed Slater -401 numerical cable to Loring Christie in London

Sir George E. Foster / Prime Minister's Office (Ottawa) to Loring C. Christie via Sir Robert L. Borden, Savoy Hotel (London) · 18 June 1918

Historical context

During the 1918 Imperial War Cabinet in London, while the German Spring Offensive strained Canadian Corps manpower to the breaking point, Ottawa cabled Borden's legal adviser Loring Christie six full unglossed pages of enciphered reinforcement statistics in Slater -401.

Decoded message (plain text)

For Christie. Corrections for memorandum from Hamilton. Detailed enciphered Canadian Expeditionary Force monthly officer and other-ranks reinforcement and casualty tables (encoding digits and ranks via Slater -401, noting figures from twenty-first to thirty-first March not available in Canada).

Original secret code

For Christie. Corrections for memorandum from Hamilton. Pennant freezes magnetic urn for turpentine attain regulated turpitude fratricidal misfortune stop Magnetic genuine regulated palaces panegyric sopping sixteenth operation pastorate referrings freezes freezes sopping epigrammatic sopping stop Magnetic usage panegyric forthwiths to turpitude fratricidal misfortune not available in Bremen stop.
