1865-03-21 · Border, Civil War & Confederation (1816-1872)

Cipher Book No. 4 wire to Grant: informant from Thompson and Tucker's Canada circle on Lee-Johnston junction and giving up Richmond (65%)

War Department (Washington, D.C.) to Lt. Gen. Ulysses S. Grant, City Point (via S. H. Beckwith) · 1865-03-21

65% decoded · Partial solveCommunity breakthrough · @jfedorGovernment & Politics

Historical context

Sent three weeks before Lincoln's assassination: an informant in confidential communication with Confederate Commissioners Jacob Thompson and Beverly Tucker in Canada reports intelligence received from Richmond up to March 11, 1865. The Confederacy had decided to unite the armies of Robert E. Lee and Joseph E. Johnston, with the junction point still undecided, and it was expected (though not yet resolved) that Richmond would be given up for that purpose. Twelve days later (April 2, 1865), Lee evacuated Petersburg and Richmond while moving toward Johnston.

Decoded message (plain text)

Washington, March 21, 1865, 11 A.M. (Harriet), 21st (Chapin), [Bladder]. A person in confidential communication (Lousy) with Thompson & Tucker, the [thumb] agents in [Garter], [whines] he has just [transports, probably = joined], that information (punish) [vixen] was (pimple) received (toppled) by them from Richmond (Hoax) to the 11th (Baldwin) inst. It had been determined to [waste] the armies (Jockeys) of Lee (Dutch) and Johnston (Drug), but at what place (squall) was still undecided. It was expected [bear] but not yet absolutely resolved, that [duplicates] army (Jockey) would [rasp] Richmond (Hidalgo) for the purpose. Signed (up) [Belgium].

Beckwith at Grant's headquarters per Stager's register, mssEC 36 p. [325]). 22 of 33 arbitrary tokens rebuilt from traffic by Jeff Fedor (@jfedor) by matching Book No. 4 sent wires (mssEC 18/19) against plaintext twins (mssEC 12/13; Lincoln to Grant, 20 March 1865): Dutch=Lee, Drug=Johnston, Hoax/Hidalgo=Richmond, toppled=received, pimple=is/was, squall=place, up/upward=Signed, Lousy=communication, tooth='to the', Jockey(s)=army/armies, punish=information, Harriet=11 A.M., Chapin=21st, Baldwin=11th, horse/Pork/Elk/bitch=period.
