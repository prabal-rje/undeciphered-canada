Awaiting Woodrow Wilson: Borden's unglossed Slater -401 secret cable on Peace Conference timing

Prime Minister Sir Robert L. Borden (London) to Prime Minister's Office / Cabinet (Ottawa) · 4 December 1918

Historical context

Three weeks after the Armistice, Prime Minister Borden sent this unglossed Slater -401 secret cablegram from London reporting that Allied consultations with French Premier Clemenceau and Italian ministers remained inconclusive pending U.S. President Woodrow Wilson's transatlantic arrival.

Decoded message (plain text)

B35 Secret. Visit of French and Italian Ministers has not yet thrown much light on probable length of our stay. After arrival of President [Wilson] will soon be determined. It will probably begin before Christmas.

Original secret code

B35 Secret Witted Pain Gilda Argyl Knowing Motioneds Horsewhipped Ordure Unable Nonchalance Lymphate On Pugilism Loutish Mef Patched Surprise Stop Anglicize Awl Pain Protecting Harriet Stinking Tub Harriet Stipulate Biting Dismal Stop It Will Probably Blindness Blend Colleague.
