Search outgoing Havana and Panama steamers for rebel hijackers and arms; commit suspects to Fort Lafayette

William H. Seward, Secretary of State (Austria) to Robert Murray, U.S. Marshal, New York · 1863-12-21

Historical context

Paired immediately with 8438 e#1 during the December 1863 maritime hijacking scare triggered by the Chesapeake seizure and Montreal/Halifax conspiracy intelligence.

Decoded message (plain text)

Washington, Dec. 21, 1863, 10 P.M. Robert Murray, U.S. Marshal. A ship of war will be stationed off Sandy Hook and another off Throg's Neck and will arrest and detain every outgoing vessel that has no pass from you. You will have your detective force in action and examine and search the outgoing steamers. The steamers the rebels will be likely to seize are the Havana and the Panama steamers. Search for arms, correspondence, and suspicious persons. Commit such persons to [Ft Humphrey]. Call on Maj. Gen. John A. Dix [Adorn] if you need aid. Secretary of State [Austria].
