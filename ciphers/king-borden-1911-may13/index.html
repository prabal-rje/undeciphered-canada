Mackenzie King warns Sir Frederick Borden at sea over a Le Devoir attack

Labour Minister W. L. Mackenzie King (Ottawa) to Militia Minister Sir Frederick Borden (S.S. Virginian), 13 May 1911

Historical context

Decoded message (plain text)

The Colonel accompanying you has written Mayor of Ottawa a letter published in papers in which he states he is leaving for England as military adviser to the Imperial Conference. Very strong criticism among our friends. Attack in Devoir. Question to be asked in House. We think it important to give prompt denial. What reply do you suggest? Cartwright has cabled Premier. King.
