23 April 1919 · Laurier & World War I (1897-1919)

Crisis and famine in India: Acting PM Thomas White cables Borden in Paris in unglossed Slater -401

Acting Prime Minister Sir Thomas White (Ottawa) to Prime Minister Sir Robert L. Borden, Hotel Astoria (Paris) · 23 April 1919

Historical context

Ten days after the Amritsar crisis and amid severe 1919 monsoon failure and food shortages across British India, Acting Prime Minister Sir Thomas White queried Borden in Paris in unglossed Slater -401 on whether Canada should organize Dominion famine relief.

Decoded message (plain text)

P.299. From White. According press despatches very grave situation exists in India owing to famine. Cable your views as to whether Dominion Government should make contribution and if so what amount and under what channels.

Original secret code

P.299. From White. According press despatches very grave situation fur feathers informal Genoa peeler uncommon flicker. Cable your views as to whether elm harmonica smells mercurially cranny and if so Jonas arbitrarily argil under Jonas clearlys.
