1864-11-29 · Border, Civil War & Confederation (1816-1872)

Inadequate guard warning for Confederate prisoners at Elmira four days after Canadian-launched New York Arson Plot

War Department (Washington, D.C.) to Maj. Gen. John A. Dix, New York (Kasson) · 1864-11-29

War & Spies

Historical context

Verified OR Series I Vol. 43/2 prior-art control.

Decoded message (plain text)

Washington, Nov. 29 [harsh pony], 1864, 2:30 P.M. [Henrietta]. Maj. Gen. Dix [Kasson]. General [Shelter] Fry is raising a regiment [whip] of reserves to send to Elmira. In the meantime [Stanhope] it is absolutely necessary [tarquin] that you furnish a proper guard [saddle] for the prisoners there. Colonel [Paradise] Tracy reports [white] that you have furnished him only [modify] men [spit], which is a force [salem] entirely inadequate.
