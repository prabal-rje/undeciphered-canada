25 June 1879 · Sir John A. Macdonald Era (1873-1896)

Tilley and Tupper sailing to London: Macdonald's interlinear +300 Slater coding sheet to Langevin

Prime Minister Sir John A. Macdonald (Ottawa) to Sir Hector-Louis Langevin, Minister of Public Works · 25 June 1879

Government & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View archival record at Library and Archives Canada ↗

Historical context

Before Finance Minister Samuel Leonard Tilley and Railways Minister Charles Tupper departed for London in June 1879 to negotiate Imperial backing for the Pacific Railway and National Policy finances, Macdonald prepared this complete 18-word Slater +300 coding worksheet to Sir Hector Langevin.

Decoded message (plain text)

Tilley sailed last Saturday. Tupper sails next Saturday. Instructions follow in cypher. Send despatch in full on receipt without fail.

Original secret code

Tilley scramble lichen securely. Tupper scraggy obtuse securely. Impropriety fruitful industrious agitation. Showerless disarray industrious gilder oxygenated register forbes fife.
