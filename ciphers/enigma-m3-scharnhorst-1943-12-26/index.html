Battleship Scharnhorst Final Kriegsmarine Enigma M3 Signal (Convoy JW 55B)

Kriegsmarine Battleship Scharnhorst to Group North · 26 December 1943

Historical context

Intercepted Kriegsmarine Home Waters (Heimisch) Enigma M3 signal during the Battle of the North Cape protecting Arctic Convoy JW 55B, the northern convoy lane patrolled alongside Royal Canadian Navy Tribal-class destroyers. Independently decrypted letter-for-letter in Python (100% character match) using rotors III-VI-VIII, Reflector B, rings A-H-M, and U-boat/surface plugboard.

Decoded message (plain text)

STEUEREJTANAFJORDJANSTANDORTQUAAACCCVIERNEUNNEUNZWOFAHRTZWONULSMXXSCHARNHORSTHCO (Final Kriegsmarine Enigma M3 radio transmission sent by the German battleship Scharnhorst on 26 December 1943 during the Battle of the North Cape (Arctic Convoy JW 55B), reporting course for Tanafjord at grid AC 4992 and speed 20 knots shortly before she was sunk.)

Original secret code

YKAE NZAP MSCH ZBFO CUVM RMDP YCOF HADZ IZME FXTH FLOL PZLF GGBO TGOX GRET DWTJ IQHL MXVJ WKZU ASTR
