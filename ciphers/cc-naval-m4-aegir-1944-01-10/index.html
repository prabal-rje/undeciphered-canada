Historical context

Authentic WWII Kriegsmarine 4-rotor Enigma M4 naval intercept (Kriegsmarine Aegir (Pike) Long-Range Submarine Net (Tokyo / Penang)). Naval Attache Tokyo to U-boat base Penang requesting an alternate replacement commander for ex-Italian submarine U-IT 23 after Fregattenkapitaen Heinrich Schaefer's death.
