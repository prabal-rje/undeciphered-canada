Decoded message (plain text)

Four-sheet rush immigration protest wire from the Secretary to the Minister of Immigration to PM Meighen in Regina regarding handling of immigrants held at Canadian ports and instructions to Canadian agents abroad (partially interlined on file).

Original secret code
