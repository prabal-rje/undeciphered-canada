Ottawa-Vancouver Secret Code Lettergram: McInnes ('Brown') Directing Inspector Foster ('Hunter') to Retain Gordon Grant

1910-09-29

Government & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View historical publication on Internet Archive ↗

Historical context

Dispatched from Ottawa on 29 September 1910 immediately after McInnes received Gordon Grant's 28 September 'JULIAN ROY' night letter from Vancouver. Using the 'Hunter' (Inspector Foster) and 'Brown' (McInnes) codewords, McInnes confirmed that Foster had been instructed to retain Grant as counsel and hire Lee Kee as an additional interpreter.

Decoded message (plain text)

Lettergram not received until afternoon. Took immediate action. Inspector Foster instructed to retain barrister Gordon Grant for to-morrow and the additional interpreters you suggest, Lee Kee. Signed T. R. E. McInnes.

Original secret code

Ottawa, September 29, 1910. David C. Lew, Chinese Interpreter, Vancouver, B.C. Lettergram not received till afternoon. Took immediate action. Hunter instructed retain Gordon Grant for to-morrow and additional interpreters you suggest Lee Kee. Brown.
