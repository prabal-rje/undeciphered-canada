5 July 1889 · Sir John A. Macdonald Era (1873-1896)

Jesuits' Estates Act crisis: Macdonald wires Governor General's Secretary Colville in Slater -1005

Prime Minister Sir John A. Macdonald (Ottawa) to Captain Charles Colville, Governor General's Secretary (New Richmond, P.Q.) · 5 July 1889

Government & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View archival record at Library and Archives Canada ↗

Historical context

At the height of Protestant Equal Rights agitation demanding federal disallowance of Quebec's Jesuits' Estates Act, Macdonald used Lord Stanley's date-keyed Slater formula (1000 + day) to advise receiving the Armour deputation and issuing a prompt reply so critics could not claim delay.

Decoded message (plain text)

Council thinks Armour's deputation should be received. Answer to Armour should be prompt.

Original secret code

Desired, vexatious, biform, ear, should, be, righting, bathos, to, biform, should, be, reconnoitred.
