11 July 1889 · Sir John A. Macdonald Era (1873-1896)

Imperial Crown Law Officers uphold Jesuits' Estates Act: Pope confirms Macdonald's Slater -1010 cable to Colville

Joseph Pope quoting Prime Minister Sir John A. Macdonald (Ottawa) to Captain Charles Colville, Governor General's Secretary (New Richmond, P.Q.) · 11 July 1889

Historical context

Reporting confidential news cabled from Sir Charles Tupper in London, Macdonald informed Governor General Lord Stanley's secretary that British Crown Law Officers were ready to confirm Ottawa's refusal to disallow Quebec's Jesuits' Estates Act once the formal despatch arrived.

Decoded message (plain text)

Law officers of Crown are ready to give favourable opinion in Jesuit matter, but await despatch. Has one sent?

Original secret code

Meagre petitions of dirk bewitch [ready] to [give] [favourable] piazza [in] [Jesuit] [matter] but bowsprit effective [has one sent].
