21 October 1925 · Interwar Ottawa (1920-1938)

Eight days before the 1925 Nova Scotia sweep: Senator W. H. Dennis's unglossed Slater -411 election forecast to Meighen

Senator William H. Dennis (Halifax, Nova Scotia) to Rt. Hon. Arthur Meighen (Stratford, Ontario) · 21 October 1925

Historical context

Eight days before the 29 October 1925 federal election, Halifax Herald publisher Senator W. H. Dennis sent this unglossed 104-word cipher forecast to Conservative leader Arthur Meighen in Stratford predicting a ten-seat Nova Scotia victory including upsets in Lunenburg and Antigonish-Guysboro (where Meighen ultimately swept 11 of 14 seats).

Decoded message (plain text)

Have completed personal survey Halifax County. Opinion is we will elect our two candidates. Impossible to accurately gauge rest of Province from reports, we will have majority of ten seats. We have fair chance to win Lunenburg from Duff, Antigonish-Guysboro from McDonald. Organization will show up well on twenty-ninth and every effort made to give victory.
