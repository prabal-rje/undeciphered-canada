1864-08-11 · Border, Civil War & Confederation (1816-1872)

Stanton warns NY Postmaster that Halifax Confederate agent Alexander Keith Jr. is in New York receiving letters from Philadelphia

Edwin M. Stanton, Secretary of War (Infant) to Abraham Wakeman, Postmaster, New York (Frog) and Operator John Horner · 1864-08-11

Historical context

Predates the 13 August Alexander Keith Jr. wire (9819) by two days and directly exposes the earlier winter 1863-64 interception of Alexander Keith Jr.'s Halifax cipher mail in New York.

Decoded message (plain text)

Washington [Grapes], Aug. 11 [Fence], 1864, 2:30 P.M. [Henrietta]. For Abraham Wakeman, Esq., Postmaster, New York [Frog]. Alexander Keith, the rebel [Walnut] agent at Halifax to whom the ciphers [Penfield] stopped by you last winter were addressed, is in New York [France]. It is likely that he is now getting letters from Philadelphia [Fool], where he has recently been, and perhaps from other points [Vernons]. His arrest [oakum] would be of immense advantage to the Government [Spark]. Can you arrange it? Signed [Yoke], Secretary of War [Infant]. To Horner [transcribed Homer]: Please examine your office business and see if anything has been received for or sent by Keith during past three days, and also look out for anything which may be received or offered in his name. He is probably in correspondence with Norris & Sons, New York [Frog]. Answer quick.
