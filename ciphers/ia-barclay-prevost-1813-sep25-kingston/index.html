25 September 1813 · New France & Early Canada (1673-1815)

Col. Thomas Barclay's encrypted warning that two American spies infiltrated Kingston

Colonel Thomas Barclay, British Agent for Prisoners (Harlem, New York) to Sir George Prevost, Governor General & Commander-in-Chief (Canada) · 25 September 1813

War & Spies
Open interactive decoder → View historical publication on Internet Archive ↗

Historical context

Hidden as an encrypted postscript at the foot of a routine prisoner-of-war letter, this counterintelligence warning alerted Canadian headquarters that two American spies had penetrated the Royal Navy base at Kingston.

Decoded message (plain text)

Two American spies have lately been in Canada, particularly at Kingston, who have obtained every information and returned.

Original secret code

[3175?] 127 650 2884 1234 650 1605 808 [265 1412 409?] 808 2184 195 1575 3470 1234 1555 1438 142 808 2584
