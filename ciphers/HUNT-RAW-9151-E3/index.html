Historical context

Encrypted 1864 War Department dispatch linking Washington to New York, Boston, New England, or Mid-Atlantic port and maritime intelligence hubs monitoring Atlantic raiders, blockade runners, counter-espionage, and northern homefront security.

Decoded message (plain text)

[1 ; John Horner Wash'n Jany 3rd 1864 12.M] [Washington] (12) [3] for [Brigadier General] Vain fleet [Quartermaster]. I [Telegraph]d last night for [Report] of Weaselira [Available] in [New York], answer not rec'd. [Colonel] Wise has called on you for Waymomers to take [1000] [Of the] construction [Corps] US military [Rail Road]'s from [Baltimore] to [Savannah]. In addition to this we are now need [Steam]ing to take [4000] [Troops] from Banditte to sea, destination not [Report]ed, should have full coal and water for [15] days. [Report] the vessels you can send and dispatch them unless counter man dead before they start [Signed] [Qr Master Genl U.S.] all sober
