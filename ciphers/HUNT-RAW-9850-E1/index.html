Historical context

Encrypted 1864 operational dispatch to or concerning the Department of the East, Northern Department, or Department of the Northwest commanders responsible for border defense and internal security.

Decoded message (plain text)

[8 PM ; Beckwith Washn Sept 25th 1864] (8 PM) [20] Dalon For [Lieut Gen U.S. Grant]. It is [Important] to remove [Heintzelman S P] from any function and perhaps [Rosecrans W S]. Have you any objection to [Hooker Jos] being assigned to the place of [1] of them? There is no vacant [Major] [General] ship for Crook but I am disposed to muster out [Heintzelman S P] and make a vacancy. Crook shall have the [1] that occurs. Duval [Harbor] promoted immedy [Signed] [Secretary of War] good
