Cipher No. 3 order releasing Confederate Canada Commissioner Clement C. Clay Jr. from Fort Monroe on parole

War Department (Washington, D.C.) to Fort Monroe, VA (via Wm. J. Dealy) · 1866-04-17

War & Spies

Historical context

Verified OR II/8 prior-art control; also serves as an additional Cipher Book No. 3 Rosetta Stone recovering 'endless' (in the), 'Sligo' (Alabama), and 'Lasted Invades' (United States).

Decoded message (plain text)

Washington, April 17, 1866. Fort Monroe. Ordered: That Clement C. Clay Jr. is hereby released from confinement and permitted to return to and remain in the State of Alabama and to visit such other places in the United States as his personal business may render absolutely necessary upon parole.

Original secret code

Wm. J. Dealy Ft Monroe Washn Apr 17th 1866 Winslow Tucker Kelly Sallie for Point Platina Clem ant See Clay Jr is here by real eased from corn fine meant and permitted to hinder to and remain endless State of Sligo and to visit such other places endless Lasted Invades as his personal business may render absolutely necessary upon the following conditions viz yellow that he takes the oath of allegiance tooth Lasted Invades and gives his parole of honor to conduct himself as a loyal citizen endless same and to report himself in person at any time and place to answer any charges that may here after be preferred against him by the Lasted Invades Potash Please harvest receipt and execution of this gender Brass Atlantic the Meriden Indigo Quitman Morphine Wonder what will come next
