1865-03-09 · Border, Civil War & Confederation (1816-1872)

Intelligence to Maj. Gen. Hooker That Confederate Navy Officers Were Relieved on the Lakes

Maj. Gen. Henry W. Halleck, General-in-Chief (Washington, D.C.) to Northern / Border Command Station · 1865-03-09

War & Spies

Historical context

March 9, 1865 Cipher Book No. 9 intelligence alert to Northern Department commander Maj. Gen. Joseph Hooker reporting that Confederate naval personnel had been relieved from duty along the northern frontier.

Decoded message (plain text)

[Cho Davenport Wash'n Mar. 9th 1865] [Washington] (4 PM) for [Jos. Hooker]. It is reported that the [Rebels] navy has been relieved from duty on the coast & sent to Western [River]'s to destroy [River] [Transportation]'s. great vigilance must be exercised to prevent such depredations sig [H. W. Halleck] End
