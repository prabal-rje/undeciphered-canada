8 February 1918 · Laurier & World War I (1897-1919)

Britain seizes Quebec-owned steamship SS Lord Ormonde over official Canadian protests

Sir George H. Perley, High Commissioner (London) to Prime Minister Sir Robert L. Borden (Ottawa), cable P49 for Marine · 8 February 1918

Historical context

Purchased in 1917 by Davie Shipbuilding of Levis, Quebec, SS Lord Ormonde became a flashpoint over Canadian maritime sovereignty when British authorities ignored Ottawa's objections. Forty days after this cable, Austro-Hungarian submarine U-40 torpedoed the ship in the Mediterranean on 20 March 1918.

Decoded message (plain text)

Summary of decoded cable P49: Ministry of Shipping states SS Lord Ormonde was requisitioned for Government service in Feb-June 1917. Owners had verified transfer from British to Canadian register (10 Feb 1917) and were told Canadian vessels would not be requisitioned without Ottawa's consent; the Director of Transports persisted despite official protests.
