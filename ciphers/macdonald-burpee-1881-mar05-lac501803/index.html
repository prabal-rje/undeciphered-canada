5 March 1881 · Sir John A. Macdonald Era (1873-1896)

Carleton County N.B. railway workshops and election patronage: handwritten +310 Slater worksheet in Macdonald's papers

Prime Minister Sir John A. Macdonald / Sir John Rose correspondence to Conservative leadership (New Brunswick / Ottawa) · 5 March 1881

Government & Politics

Historical context

Endorsed '5 March 1881, re Burpee as Managing Director N.B. Railway in Carleton Co. N.B.', this handwritten Slater +310 worksheet warns that former Liberal cabinet minister Isaac Burpee was promising to relocate New Brunswick Railway workshops to Woodstock (Carleton's county town) to swing the upcoming by-election.

Decoded message (plain text)

Carleton: Stop this. He promises removal of workshops to County Town of Carleton to carry election.

Original secret code

Carleton survey trifling hoof puncture restrictive overlooked Harriet slatternlys type cumulative unbind overlooked Carleton type char enterprising.
