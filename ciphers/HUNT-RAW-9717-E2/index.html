Historical context

Encrypted 1864 War Department dispatch linking Washington to New York, Boston, New England, or Mid-Atlantic port and maritime intelligence hubs monitoring Atlantic raiders, blockade runners, counter-espionage, and northern homefront security.

Decoded message (plain text)

[John Horner NY Wash'n Apr. 22nd 1864 10 PM] [Washington] April Twenty Second (10 PM) For [Major] Van West [Quartermaster] [New York]. Enough [Steam Boats] and Propellers. Complete the supply of tugs of Ferry boats of barges and Schooners for both [(Fort) Monroe] and [Washington]. There will be much material to move. Steamers Enough to move [Troops] are now Engaged. Devote yourself to Expediting the arrival of vessels at their destined ports. I fear delay which would be most injurious sig M C Meigs [Quartermaster General]
