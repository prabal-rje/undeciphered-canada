Historical context

October 14, 1864 Cipher Book No. 1 trans-Pacific diplomatic relay transmitted through San Francisco to Secretary of State William H. Seward reporting negotiations involving the U.S. Legation at Kanagawa.

Decoded message (plain text)

[WJ Dealey Ft Monroe Washn Oct 15th 1864] San Francisco [California] [14] for [Secretary of State]. U S Legation Kanawaga [Sept 84] Sir. The ambassadors who made the French convention have been disgraced Convention repudiated Combined fleet gone to [Destroy] [Battery]'s Simonski yours &c Robt H. Pruyn Minister resident [Maj Genl U.S. Grant] another from Rector town [15] for [General-in-Chief]. The train from [Alexandria] has arrived and [Report]ed on time [Today] and without interference from guerillas Nothing heard from [P. H. Sheriden] [Today] signed [Major] [General] Augur deliver also copy of [Nashville] [Cipher]
