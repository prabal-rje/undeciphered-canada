12 October 1921 · Interwar Ottawa (1920-1938)

Tracing "Lynton's Code" into a B.C. riding fight

Geo. F. Buskard (PMO) to Hon. S. F. Tolmie (Burns Lake, B.C.) · 12 October 1921

Unread until nowGovernment & Politics
Open interactive decoder → View original scan at Library and Archives Canada / Héritage Canadiana ↗

Historical context

Meighen's office files pointed repeatedly to "Lynton's code." Parliamentary records from 1922 identify Thomas J. Lynton as Agriculture Minister Tolmie's private secretary, and applying the office note's shift of 40 (#027220) unlocks every line of a local riding quarrel over Colonel Peck in Skeena and Comox.

Decoded message (plain text)

“Your joint wire with Stevens. Stop. I understand favoured Peck but this inconsistent with recent wire from Stevens if I understand them right. Stop. Also have no information what is to harmonize in Skeena if Peck chosen and would think probably danger with Comox candidate also. Stop. Wire more fully situation through Lynton.”

Original secret code

Your joint wire with Stevens stop I uneasy feebly pelican but this inconsistent woof recognisable woman fagleman Stevens... whirlpool jaded to harmonize in Skeena illusionist pelican churlish and would think procured deacon woof Comox canteen also stop
