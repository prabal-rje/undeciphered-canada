# Undeciphered.ca

**Charting Canada's history through its ciphers (1673-1945).**

Live site: [https://undeciphered.ca](https://undeciphered.ca)

`Undeciphered.ca` is an independent, non-commercial historical cryptography project maintained by **Prabal Gupta**. It indexes **859** genuine raw coded letters, telegrams, and wartime intercepts from Canadian history (`854` solved, `5` open or provisional challenges, including **219** previously unread archival ciphertexts), plus an opt-in catalog of **2,035** published historical cipher summaries.

## Important Disclaimer for Readers & Researchers

> **Independent non-commercial project (provided AS-IS, no liability):** Transcriptions, raw ciphertexts, and decoded readings on `Undeciphered.ca` are provided for historical, educational, and research interest only and may contain transcription errors or archival caveats. Readers, journalists, and scholars should always verify every ciphertext and reading directly against the linked original holding archive before citing or relying on it. See [LICENSE](LICENSE) for full attribution terms and disclaimer of warranties and liability.

Third-party archival document scans are **not** hosted in this repository. Every entry links directly to its official repository viewer (Library and Archives Canada / Heritage Canadiana, Archives nationales d'outre-mer, Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec, The Huntington Library, Hudson's Bay Company Archives, University of Ottawa, and Internet Archive). Background photographs and period plates derive solely from verified Public Domain and CC0 museum photographs.

## Contributing Ciphertexts, Keys & Solutions (Issues Only, No Code PRs)

Historians, archivists, amateur codebreakers, and independent researchers are warmly invited to help discover, transcribe, decode, and verify Canadian historical ciphers!

- **What we accept (via GitHub Issues ONLY):**
  - Raw Canadian historical ciphertexts with an official public archive link and shelfmark
  - Deterministic keys, codebook shifts, substitution tables, and full or partial decodings
  - Transcription corrections, prior scholarly credits, and historical context updates
- **What we do NOT accept:**
  - **Code contributions and Pull Requests are prohibited.** Please do not submit HTML, CSS, JavaScript, build scripts, or automated PRs; unsolicited Pull Requests will be closed without review.

Every submission is verified by hand against the official archive. Accepted contributors are credited on the cipher's own **Contribution & Decode Timeline** (showing role and percentage solved) and on the **Community Leaderboard** at [https://undeciphered.ca/contribute/](https://undeciphered.ca/contribute/) within **1 to 2 weeks**.

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the scoring guide, verification rules, and LLM-ready GitHub Issue JSON schema.
