Interwar Ottawa & Prime Ministerial Ciphers (1920-1938)

In the 1920s, Prime Minister Arthur Meighen pushed Slater's pocket codebook to its limit with alternating multi-key shifts to shield judicial patronage wires and Grain Inquiry correspondence, before a 1936 Prime Minister's Office memo finally retired the book.
