Sir John A. Macdonald Era Secret Telegrams (1873-1896)

Starting in the 1870s, Prime Minister Sir John A. Macdonald and his ministers adopted shifted word lookups from an 1870 London pocket dictionary (Slater's Telegraphic Code) to conceal railway negotiations, Prairie security orders, Bering Sea talks, and election wires.
